# Changelog

Every version keeps the content of the versions before it. Results from different engine builds are
kept in separate archives and tables and are never merged.

## v1.1.0 (2026-11-22)

Primary build: **vtop-engine v0.10.4** (be5b419), the build the paper reports (decision D17). Archived builds, kept
side by side and never merged with it: **v0.10.2** (0abf116) and **v0.7.0** (6c8f386). The v0.7.0 results, archives,
tables and figures of v1.0.4 are unchanged; the v0.7.0 result archives hold the same files as in v1.0.4, re-packed with
the owner `root` (two archives touched by the redaction, see below). The v0.10.2
results stay because the paper cites v0.10.2 to v0.10.4 comparisons (the RQ3 A/B slowdown, the tamper verdict changes).

Engine pins for v0.10.4 (how they were checked is in the README): tag be5b419; archives
`vtop-engine-0.10.4-aarch64-apple-darwin.tar.gz` SHA-256 `fc132e7c…9b71` and
`vtop-engine-0.10.4-aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz` SHA-256 `0266c37b…7c84`, both matching the release `SHA256SUMS`
(file SHA-256 `90e807f5…0ff4`, included as `docs/vtop-engine-0.10.4-SHA256SUMS`); cosign verified.
